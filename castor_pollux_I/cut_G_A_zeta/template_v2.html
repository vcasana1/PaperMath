Where G, A and ζ meet

G(s) = ∫1∞ log|2 sin πu| u−s−1 du and the auxiliary function A of Castor and Pollux I, Proposition 14.1. Proposition 17.1 of version 5 proves the identity below. For non-real s, G(s) = A(s) exactly when ζ(s) = 0, and at those points G and A are equal but not zero: they meet at a common nonzero value.

G(s) − A(s) = (π/s) · cot(πs/2) · ζ(s)
